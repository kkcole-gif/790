Milestone 2 · Due before class, Week 8 (March 4) · 15% of the final grade

Committee Confirmation

The faculty you ask will shape your project, your development as a scholar, and your relation to the field for years. This milestone comes before the graded exam list, so the list answers to the people who will read it. It asks you to show the process is underway. Your committee need not be final.

What to submit

Submit one of two options. They describe different stages of the same process, and either meets the threshold when it is honest. Faculty replies in February run slow, and a credible plan counts as complete.

Option A: your chair’s confirmation

Forward or paste the email in which your chair agrees to serve. A brief reply suffices.

Option B: a written plan

  • Named faculty for each position: chair, readers, and an outside member if your program requires one.
  • The status of each conversation: asked, waiting to hear, or planning to ask by a named date.
  • A realistic timeline for completing the committee.

Questions to prepare for your meeting

These questions prepare you for the Week 8 workshop and the Week 10 meeting. They describe a strong committee, and the milestone does not grade them.

QuestionA strong answerA weaker answer
Intellectual fitEach member brings something the project needs: a method, a tradition, a subject, an outside discipline.Members chosen because they are available, collegial, or likely to say yes.
Productive challengeAt least one member will push back in ways which improve the work.A committee certain to agree with you.
Your goalsIf your goals include work beyond the academy, the committee speaks to those paths too.A committee oriented only to the academic job market, whatever your plans.
First contactYou have spoken with your chair, or scheduled the conversation.Faculty identified but not yet contacted, without a dated plan.

If you hit obstacles

A faculty member has not replied. You are unsure whom to ask. An advising relationship is difficult. You do not know how to make first contact. Say so in your submission. I reply within the week, and we return to it in your Week 10 meeting. Silence is the one response which leaves the problem in place.

Assessment

Complete and substantive
A chair’s confirmation, or a plan with named faculty, the status of each conversation, and a specific timeline.
Nearly there
A plan with unnamed faculty or a vague timeline.
Not yet complete
Nothing submitted and no word about obstacles. Come talk to me.
