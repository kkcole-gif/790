Your draft list is the reading this week. A good list makes an argument about a field and your place in it, and a reader from outside your project should be able to see the argument.

Before we meet

Milestone due: Committee Confirmation

Either an email from your chair confirming their willingness to serve, or a written plan naming faculty for each position, the status of each conversation, and a realistic timeline. Faculty replies in February run slow, so a credible plan meets the threshold. If you have hit obstacles, such as a faculty member who has not replied or uncertainty about whom to ask, say so in the submission. 15% of the final grade. Submit through Canvas or by email.

Full guide to the Committee Confirmation milestone

Bring

  • Your draft list and rationale, ready to trade with a partner.
  • One list from the folder of past CRDM exam lists whose organization you admire or would argue with.
  • Any responses from prospective committee members.

Questions for peer review

  • What intellectual logic holds the list together: theme, method, genealogy, intervention?
  • Where are the temporal, geographic, or demographic gaps? Which fields or traditions are absent?
  • Does the list reflect what the field values, what you value, or what your committee values, and do the three agree?
  • Which fields will read this dissertation, and from what position does the list enter each?
  • Is there a text on the list you have not read? What would a list you wanted to read look like?

Structured peer review

Pairs are assigned. Each reader responds in writing before any discussion begins.

  1. Exchange lists and rationale paragraphs.
  2. Respond in writing for fifteen minutes: the organizing logic I see, a gap I notice, a text I would add and why, the most interesting tension on the list, and one question for the author about the choices behind it.
  3. Discuss the responses, ten minutes per list.
  4. Join the full group to compare what the lists reveal about the cohort, and set them beside the past CRDM exam lists.
