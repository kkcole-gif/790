Movement III · Week 15 · Thursday, April 22

What We Made and What We’re Seeing

Our last session. You present your work to colleagues, and the cards from our first day come back. Come proud of what you have made, and honest about what you are still working out.

Kirsti

Each of you presents your project to colleagues: what you are doing, why it matters, and what you are still working out. The session is a celebration with teeth, honest and collegial, and it ends where the semester began.

Before we meet

Prepare

  • A five to ten minute presentation of your prospectus. Slides are optional. Bring notes if they help. Speak to colleagues about work you care about.

Questions to guide your presentation

  • What problem are you working on, and why does it matter beyond your field?
  • Who are your interlocutors, the scholars you build on and the ones you argue against?
  • What methodological move are you making, and what does it let you see?
  • What are you still working out?
  • Whom is this for, and what do you hope it does in the world?

Card return and closing reflection

The constellation cards from Week 1 come back.

  1. Read what you wrote in Week 1 beside what you presented today.
  2. Write for five minutes in silence: what changed, what held, and what question you carry forward.
  3. Go around the room. Each of you offers one sentence, the one thing you take out of the room.

After the session

  • Submit your final prospectus one week after class, through Canvas or by email.
  • Optional: reread your Week 5 Identity Statement and write a 200-word addendum. What would you add, revise, or complicate now?
