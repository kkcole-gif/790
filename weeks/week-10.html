Before your meeting

Milestone due: Draft Exam List

Your exam list with a one-paragraph rationale, revised after Week 8 peer review, the Week 9 canon audit, and your committee’s responses. The rationale states the argument of the list: why these texts, in this configuration, for this project. It also names the fields your dissertation will answer to and the position from which you enter each one. A disciplinary list locates a position within one field. An interdisciplinary list maps where several fields meet your problem and states which field claims the project first. 25% of the final grade. Submit through Canvas, by email, or in hard copy.

Full guide to the Draft Exam List milestone

Write and bring

  • A support constellation, a companion to the map you drew in Week 1. The first map placed you among ideas. This one places you among the people, places, and practices which sustain your work: mentors, peers, family, a place you write well, a practice which restores you. Mark who you will check in with between now and your exams. Name a writing partner from the cohort, or ask me to pair you.
  • Prospectus seed writing, 300 to 500 words, unedited: “The problem my dissertation addresses is ___, and it matters because ___.” We read it together in the meeting.
  • Your revised exam list, with the addition and cut from Week 9.

Questions to think through

  • What has changed in how you think about your research question since Week 1?
  • Where is your exam list still unresolved, and what would resolve it?
  • What problem will your dissertation address, in one sentence?
  • Who is your committee, and what does their assembly tell you about what your project needs?
  • What are you most afraid your prospectus will reveal about your project?

The meeting

Office hours are limited March 11 to 24. Open my calendar with your NC State account, or search for Kirsti Cole in Google Calendar, then send me an invitation for an open 30-minute slot. Meetings open with your committee contacts, move to the list, and end with your seed writing. Zoom works if you are away.

After your meeting

  • Revise one sentence from your seed writing, the one now truer or clearer, and keep it. It might open your prospectus.
  • Rest over spring break. Read something for pleasure.
  • Starting Week 11, the writing commons opens: an optional writing hour after class on Thursdays through April 22, with the time and room posted in Canvas. Trade one page a week with your writing partner, by Monday, and send back one sentence on what the page does well and one question.
