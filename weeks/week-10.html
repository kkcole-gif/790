Movement II · Week 10 · Individual meetings, March 11 to 24

Individual Meetings

No group session this week. Each of you meets with me for twenty to thirty minutes on your list, your committee, and the problem your dissertation addresses.

When
By appointment, March 11 to 24, skipping spring break
Bring
Your revised list and 300 to 500 words of prospectus seed writing
Due
Milestone 3, Committee Confirmation, by March 24

Before your meeting

Milestone due: Committee Confirmation

Either an email from your chair confirming their willingness to serve, or a written plan naming faculty for each position, the status of each conversation, and a realistic timeline. If you have hit obstacles, such as a faculty member who has not replied or uncertainty about whom to ask, say so in the submission, and we will work on it in your meeting. 15% of the final grade. Submit through Canvas or by email.

Write and bring

  • Prospectus seed writing, 300 to 500 words, unedited: “The problem my dissertation addresses is ___, and it matters because ___.” We read it together in the meeting.
  • Your revised exam list, with the addition and cut from Week 9.

Questions to think through

  • What has changed in how you think about your research question since Week 1?
  • Where is your exam list still unresolved, and what would resolve it?
  • What problem will your dissertation address, in one sentence?
  • Who is your committee, and what does their assembly tell you about what your project needs?
  • What are you most afraid your prospectus will reveal about your project?

The meeting

Book a slot through Canvas. Meetings open with your committee contacts, move to the list, and end with your seed writing. Zoom works if you are away.

After your meeting

  • Revise one sentence from your seed writing, the one now truer or clearer, and keep it. It might open your prospectus.
  • Rest over spring break. Read something for pleasure.
