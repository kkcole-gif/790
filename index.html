Milestones

The course uses threshold grading. Each milestone is assessed as complete and substantive, nearly there, or not yet complete. Submit through Canvas, by email, or in hard copy.

  • Scholarly Identity Statement, due Week 5 (Feb 4), before class. 20% of the final grade. About 500 words on who you are as a scholar and what your research is for.
  • Draft Exam List, due Week 8 (Feb 25), before class. 25% of the final grade. A working list with a rationale naming the fields it answers to and your position in each.
  • Committee Confirmation, due Week 10 (Mar 11), during the week. 15% of the final grade. Your chair's confirmation, or a plan naming faculty and a timeline.
  • Dissertation Prospectus, due in two stages: a workshop draft before Week 14 (Apr 15), and the final version one week after Week 15 (Apr 29). 40% of the final grade. A problem, its place in the scholarship, a method, and stakes beyond the field. Nonacademic equivalents are welcome.

Access and comfort

Every session page and handout comes in more than one format. If a format creates a barrier for you, email me, and you will have an alternative before the next session. If you hold an accommodation letter, share it with me in the first two weeks.

Sessions run close to three hours. Bring food. Leave the room whenever you need to, without asking. You are welcome here as a whole person.
