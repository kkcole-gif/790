Schedule

Each week page lists what to read and bring, how the session runs, and what follows. Current students, alumni, and program faculty join us across the term.

Movement I · Weeks 1 to 5

Who Are We and What Do We Think?

A shared critical vocabulary, and a first account of who each of you is as a scholar. Readings from Giroux, Benjamin, Mbembe, and Brown.

  • Week 1Jan 14ArrivalsIntroductions, intellectual autobiographies, constellation mapping.
  • Week 2Jan 21Critical Pedagogy and the Scholar-CriticGiroux, On Critical Pedagogy. Institutional autopsy.
  • Week 3Jan 28The Past Is Not PastGuest Benjamin, Illuminations. Wreckage and promise gallery. A current doctoral student joins us.
  • Week 4Feb 4Who Gets to LiveMbembe, Necropolitics. Concept translation workshop.

Committees, exam lists, and the labor behind both. Readings from Federici, Coverley, and Haraway.

Milestones

The course uses threshold grading. Each milestone is assessed as complete and substantive (full points), nearly there (80 percent), or not yet complete (no points). A nearly-there milestone revised to the complete threshold earns its full points. Submit through Canvas, by email, or in hard copy.

  • Scholarly Identity Statement, due Week 5 (Feb 11), before class. 20% of the final grade. About 500 words on who you are as a scholar and what your research is for.
  • Draft Exam List, due Week 8 (Mar 4), before class. 25% of the final grade. A working list with a rationale naming the fields it answers to and your position in each.
  • Committee Confirmation, due by March 24, the close of the Week 10 meeting window. 15% of the final grade. Your chair's confirmation, or a plan naming faculty and a timeline.
  • Dissertation Prospectus, due in two stages: a workshop draft before Week 14 (Apr 15), and the final version by 11:00 am on Apr 29, the close of our scheduled exam slot. 40% of the final grade. A problem, its place in the scholarship, a method, and stakes beyond the field. Nonacademic equivalents are welcome.

Access and comfort

Every session page and handout comes in more than one format. If a format creates a barrier for you, email me, and you will have an alternative before the next session. If you hold an accommodation letter, share it with me in the first two weeks.

Sessions run close to three hours. Bring food. Leave the room whenever you need to, without asking. You are welcome here as a whole person.
